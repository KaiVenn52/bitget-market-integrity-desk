# Manual Demo Recording Script — 2:30 Target

Use this as the primary judge-facing walkthrough. Record the real production site in one continuous take. No face camera is required.

## Recording setup

- Record at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, landscape.
- Use Chrome or Edge in a clean private window at 90–100% zoom.
- Hide bookmarks, downloads, extensions, personal tabs, desktop icons, and notifications.
- Use a visible mouse pointer and move it slowly. Do not add artificial cursor effects.
- Record clear English voice. Do not use background music unless it is almost inaudible.
- Open `https://bitget-market-integrity-desk.vercel.app` before recording and refresh once.
- Best recording window: a weekday after the U.S. market opens, approximately 21:35–23:30 Malaysia time while daylight saving time is active.
- Use this exact question: `Can I trust Apple's rToken price right now, and what evidence is still missing?`
- Keep the take only if the completed result shows `LIVE · QWEN`. If it returns `SNAPSHOT · RULES`, reload and record another take.
- Do not read exact prices from this script. Let the live screen supply them.

## Shot-by-shot operation and narration

### 0:00–0:15 — Open on Desk

**Action:** Start with the full page visible. Keep the cursor still near the title, then move toward the three production-proof layers.

**Say:**

> Tokenized U.S. equities can trade around the clock, but their underlying markets, reference feeds, and information timelines do not. A price difference alone is not an explanation.

### 0:15–0:28 — Introduce the product

**Action:** Point briefly to `Bitget rToken`, `Stock+ reference`, and `Qwen investigator`. Do not click the external links.

**Say:**

> Market Integrity Desk turns that uncertainty into an auditable Market State Passport. It retrieves evidence, runs deterministic checks, and uses Qwen only for bounded synthesis.

### 0:28–0:48 — Submit a natural-language task

**Action:** Click the Research question field, select all existing text, type `Can I trust Apple's rToken price right now, and what evidence is still missing?`, then click `Investigate`. Leave the progress message visible while it runs.

**Say:**

> I will ask a natural-language question about Apple. The resolver maps Apple to rAAPLUSDT, checks Bitget market data and the Stock+ reference, then sends only the recorded evidence to Qwen. The progress message exposes that handoff instead of hiding it behind a spinner.

### 0:48–1:05 — Read the result contract

**Action:** When the result loads, point to the `LIVE · QWEN` mode label, then the Market State Passport verdict and the three primary facts. Do not claim that the state must be PASS; show whatever live result appears.

**Say:**

> The mode label matters. Live identifies the data path. Qwen identifies a brief that passed the evidence-citation contract. The verdict below is derived from observable checks, not from model confidence.

### 1:05–1:22 — Prove deterministic arithmetic

**Action:** Expand `Price alignment`. Hold long enough for the formula, threshold, token value, reference value, and spread to be readable.

**Say:**

> Price alignment is deterministic. The published formula compares the token with its underlying in basis points, using a twenty-basis-point pass boundary. Qwen never performs this arithmetic.

### 1:22–1:36 — Prove temporal checks

**Action:** Close Price alignment if needed and expand `Quote freshness`. Point to the separate source ages and thresholds.

**Say:**

> Freshness is also checked per source. A recent token quote cannot make a stale underlying quote fresh. The desk preserves those timestamps separately.

### 1:36–1:58 — Show honest failure states and provenance

**Action:** Scroll just enough to show `Corporate actions` and `Liquidity observability`. Expand one of them. In the Evidence panel, select a source item, then click `Inspect provenance` and hold on the raw record.

**Say:**

> Missing evidence never becomes a negative fact. Corporate context can remain Unverifiable, and unavailable depth remains Not Observable. Every item keeps its provider, endpoint, capture time, state, and raw provenance.

### 1:58–2:13 — Explain Qwen's role

**Action:** Scroll to the AI evidence brief. Point to `Question answered`, then click one bracketed evidence citation so the corresponding evidence item becomes selected.

**Say:**

> Qwen answers the exact submitted question, but it may cite only evidence IDs supplied by the application. If those citations fail validation, the product falls back to deterministic rules instead of presenting unsupported AI text.

### 2:13–2:26 — Show Method

**Action:** Click `Method` in the top navigation. Move the pointer across Observe, Verify, Investigate, and Abstain.

**Say:**

> The workflow is Observe, Verify, Investigate, and Abstain. This is an AI research desk where the human decides, not a price oracle and not an order-execution bot.

### 2:26–2:43 — Show Replay evidence

**Action:** Click `Replay`, then click `Inspect protocol`. Hold on the four headline metrics and the disclosed protocol note.

**Say:**

> Replay Lab publishes sixteen frozen cases across four instruments. This first slice measures safe abstention when the reference is missing. It reports valid citations and zero directional claims, not fabricated trading performance.

### 2:43–2:52 — Close

**Action:** Click `Desk` and end on the full product view. Keep the URL visible for at least three seconds.

**Say:**

> The question is not whether AI can sound confident. It is whether the system can prove what it knew, when it knew it, and what it could not verify.

## Retake conditions

Discard the take and record again if any of these occur:

- A personal notification, email, wallet address, API key, or unrelated tab appears.
- The site shows `SNAPSHOT · RULES` for the main demonstration.
- Qwen takes longer than roughly 25 seconds and pushes the video beyond three minutes.
- The cursor hides important values or moves rapidly back and forth.
- Exact live values are spoken incorrectly.
- The recording is below 1080p, text is blurred, or microphone audio clips.

# Demo Video Creative Brief

## Decision

A 42-second 16:9 Twitter/X product clip using **Problem → Demo → Proof → CTA**. The viewer is a Bitget hackathon judge who already understands trading but needs immediate proof that this is not another generic stock chatbot. The single action is to open the live demo.

## Visual direction

- **Tone:** authoritative, evidence-led, restrained.
- **Palette:** product graphite `#091017`, panel `#0d161f`, mint `#50e3b4`, amber `#f8bf47`, coral `#ff6b72`, blue `#71bfff`.
- **Type:** Inter-style system sans plus IBM Plex Mono-style monospace.
- **Motion:** smooth fade-up and depth shift only; no bounce, cyberpunk effects, or decorative trading charts.
- **Sound:** low-volume generated ambient tone; the clip remains fully understandable muted.

## Storyboard and frame briefs

1. **Hook, 0–5s — hero.** Focal point: “24/7 markets. Partial information.” Dark radial depth, mint evidence line.
2. **Problem, 5–11s — split.** Focal point: “A price gap is not an explanation.” Token time, underlying time, and news time arrive separately.
3. **Product, 11–25s — browser frame.** The real implementation screenshot occupies 70%; three staggered annotations: deterministic checks, explicit unknowns, source provenance.
4. **Integrity boundary, 25–32s — data frame.** `LIVE · RULES`, `UNVERIFIABLE`, and `NOT OBSERVABLE` show that partial evidence stays partial.
5. **Proof, 32–37s — architecture.** Observe → Verify → Investigate → Abstain, plus “8/8 tests” and “Public live rToken.” Metrics are observed developer QA only.
6. **CTA, 37–42s — clean close.** “Try the live evidence desk” with the stable Vercel URL. One action, five-second hold.

## Preflight

- One focal point per scene; screenshot is inside browser chrome.
- No text block exceeds eight words per line.
- Headline ≥56px, supporting copy ≥28px.
- Crossfades provide dwell and continuity; animations are frame-driven and clamped.
- One effect family: depth shift with restrained glow emphasis.
- Final CTA holds five seconds.
- No unverified adoption, performance, or trading claims.

# X Project Post Draft

Tokenized equities trade 24/7. Their evidence doesn't.

Over 14 days of rTSLA, 51.5% of the hours when the U.S. market was closed saw the token drift more than 20 bps from its last verified close — and the biggest overnight repricing (478 bps) was real information, not noise. Nobody can tell which is which in advance.

So I built a desk that doesn't guess.

Market Integrity Desk measures the repricing, finds where it began, pulls timestamped headlines, and ranks them against that moment:

→ POSSIBLE — published 3m before the move
→ TIMING REJECTED — published 26m after the move began

Qwen writes the explanation, but only from evidence the server retrieved, and every claim has to cite a resolvable evidence ID. If nothing matches the timing, it says NO_STRONG_CATALYST instead of inventing a cause.

AI explains the move. The integrity layer proves what supports it.
